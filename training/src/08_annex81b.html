Salary Transfer Modes and Requirements

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 19
  1. Salary credit by remittance from unknown source is not acceptable unless satisfactory supporting documents is provided
  2. Salary variance within 20% (+/-) is acceptable. However, if it is > 20% (+/-) then documentary proof such as pay slip, company's letter etc. may be required for a review. CPV confirmation in lieu of documentary proof is acceptable if positive variance is due to performance/ regular bonus or negative variance in salary was during customer's vacation/leave period. However, for deals which will skip DDEQC queue based on pre-defined rules, eligibility will be calculated using the lowest of last 3 months' salary
  3. Pay slip is an added supporting document to an income document (SC/STL) based on which RCD analyzes the case. Pay slip shall be accepted at RAO stage as long as it is identical to the one available in Finnone as a scan image. However, if RCD has updated special instructions to provide pay slip in original or with stamp then source must comply with RCD instructions
WORKED EXAMPLE – SALARY VARIANCE
Last 3 salaries: 10,000 / 11,500 / 10,200 (reference salary 10,000) Highest variance = (11,500 − 10,000) ÷ 10,000 = +15% → within 20% ✔ acceptable Last 3 salaries: 10,000 / 13,000 / 10,000 Variance = +30% → more than 20% → pay slip / company letter may be required (or CPV confirmation if the extra was a performance / regular bonus) DDEQC-skip deal: eligibility uses LOWEST of last 3 = AED 10,000

Irregular Salary

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 19
  1. STL and LS: The mismatch of salary dates check will be done on last 3 salaries. If two salaries in last 3 months, are on same date or within +/- 5 days then check on third salary is not required
  2. Others: The mismatch of salary dates check will be done on last 3 salaries. If two salaries in last 3 months, are on same date or within +/- 3 days then third salary is acceptable if it is within +/- 7 days from any salary
  3. Advance/early salary payments by employer or by the governments are not considered as irregular salary

Joint Finance & Guarantors

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 19
Joint Finance & Guarantors: Allowed under STL TML segment only with following relations:
  • Husband/wife / Parents / Brothers/sisters / Son/daughter.
  • Also, joint financing should comply with following:
  • Joint customer profile to be opened in the system
  • The joint finance will guarantee the Murayama agreement
  • Prime finance must have his/her salary transfer to the DIB and has to provide the SIO
  • PDC customers transferring salary to non- DIB joint account acceptable subject to NOC provided by joint account holder
Guarantors liability: Please refer to Income Assessment and DSR Computation Policy
WORKED EXAMPLE
Customer AU (STL, TML Cat A) wants a joint PF with the spouse → allowed: open a joint customer profile; the joint party guarantees the agreement; the prime applicant's salary is transferred to DIB. A joint PF with a cousin → not allowed (relation not listed). A joint PF for a Non-TML customer → not allowed (STL TML only).

Long Tenure Finance

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 19
If customer has a facility with remaining tenure more than MPT [1] (maximum permissible tenure prescribed by CBUAE for Personal Finance or Home Finance [2]) then no consumer finance is to be granted. However, PF with remaining tenure MPT and the same is being settled with a new finance within CB regulation of multiple, DSR. Bank needs to ensure that facility with remaining tenure more than MPT is being settled with fresh finance. This is allowed for both UAE nationals and Expats. Expats are allowed with below conditions
  1. Income>=10,000 AED per month
  2. Last restructure not done in the last 12 months
  3. No current overdue on both DIB and AECB facilities
  4. Never 30+DPD on any facilities with DIB & AECB in the last 12M
  5. Meets CBUAE norms of maximum tenor, DBR, tenure, maximum finance multiples and DIB maximum finance amount for the new facility
  6. The facility with remaining tenor > 48M must be settled
  7. No deviation on above parameters
[1] MPT = Personal Finance, 60 months for MOD, 48 months for Non-MOD. Home Finance 300 months
[2] Fresh finance can be extended to customers having government backed housing program's facility with tenure more than 300 months
WORKED EXAMPLE
Customer AV, Expat, non-MOD, income AED 12,000 Existing PF at another bank restructured 2 years ago: remaining tenure 56 months (> MPT 48) Check i Income ≥ 10,000 ✔ Check ii Last restructure > 12 months ago ✔ Check iii No current overdue (DIB & AECB) ✔ Check iv Never 30+ DPD in last 12 months ✔ Check v New PF within 48 months, 20x, DBR 50%, DIB max finance ✔ Check vi The 56-month facility is settled by the new finance ✔ Result: allowed, with NO deviation on any of the above
If the same customer earned AED 9,000 → not allowed (Expat income below 10,000) and no deviation can be approved.

Special Profession, Special Needs and Restrictions

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 19
Special Profession: Foreign and Local diplomats, Member of ruling families, Lawyers, Auditors, Judges are considered as special profession & shall be approved by Head of Initiation & above
Customer with special needs: Deaf and mute customer – To provide POA duly notarized by court or notary public authorizing an assistant/agent to act on behalf of the customer for banking transactions including banking facilities
Restrictions:
Bedouin/Stateless: Restricted
With Marsoom: Restricted
Domestic Workers: Under individual visa (such as cooks, drivers, etc.) are restricted
Financial Institution Outsource Staff. Restricted in all segments
Outsource: Non TML not allowed
WATCH OUT – RESTRICTED PROFILES

Restricted

  • Bedouin / Stateless
  • Customers "with Marsoom"
  • Domestic workers on individual visa (cooks, drivers…)
  • Financial-institution outsourced staff (all segments)
  • Outsourced staff of Non-TML companies

Special handling

  • Diplomats, ruling-family members, lawyers, auditors, judges → approval by Head of Initiation & above
  • Deaf & mute customers → notarised POA for an assistant/agent
