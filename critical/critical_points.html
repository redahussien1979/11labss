Personal Finance – 71 Critical Points

DIB Personal Finance Operating Credit Policy (ref. 2026-07-0005 | 002306) + Credit Circular effective 15 Feb 2026 · All amounts in AED · Internal

A. Employer categories & segments

  1. TML categories: A, B, C, D. Employer not on the list = Non-TML (NTML).
  2. STL segment: salary with DIB, TML A, B, C, STL in DIB format. DIB staff = STL Cat A.
  3. Salaried/Privileged: salary with DIB, TML Cat D or Non-TML.
  4. PDC: salary in another bank – TML (A–D) and Non-TML.
  5. 3 salary transfers. STL & Salaried/Privileged: latest must be in DIB. PDC: 3 salaries in 1 or more banks.
  6. First-salary waiver – only Cat A/B/C with STL, if one applies: HR-CPV confirms next salary to DIB · STL/SC verified on government portal · new to country · job change with gap ≤ 121 days · salaries via exchange house.
  7. Eligible BFI (banks & financial institutions) = TML Cat B.
  8. Employer remarks codes: code 8 (suspended) restricted for everyone · code 2 Expats restricted for all risk levels (locals normal) · codes 1 & 7 Expat High risk auto-reject · code 6 HR CPV mandatory.

B. Minimum salary

  1. DIB salary: TML A/B/C 3,000 · TML D 5,000 · Non-TML 5,000.
  2. PDC: 5,000 for TML and Non-TML.
  3. Asset cross sell: assessed income ≥ 8,000.
  4. Auto Sukuk Pack: 3,000 · PDC Non-TML 10,000.
  5. BFI employees:

  9. BFI: with STL commitment UAEN 5M / Expat 3M · without: UAEN 800K TML / 200K NTML; Expat 300K TML / 150K NTML.
  10. Minimum finance: 10K fresh · 5K top-up.

D. DBR, multiples, tenure (CBUAE – no deviation)

  1. DBR: 50% salary · 30% pension. Pension + salary: Finnone uses 60% pension + 100% salary.
  2. No DBR for finance against TD / cash secured.
  3. DIB staff: DBR calculated by DIB-HR.
  4. Multiples: 20x · 15x MOD.
  5. Max tenure: 48 months · 60 MOD.
  6. Min tenure: 6 months (shorter only if secured by investment).
  7. Eligible amount = LOWEST of multiples cap, DBR cap, segment maximum.

F. Age

  1. Minimum 21 · 18 if cash-secured.
  2. UAE Nationals 18–21: DIB salary only, lower of 10x or 250K. PDC not allowed.
  3. Max age at maturity: Expat 60 (65 if income ≥ 30K) · UAE National 65 (75 pension only) · Private Banking 75 · Against TD UAE National > 75 and < 85 → 12-month tenure only.

G. AECB & payment history

  1. AECB auto-reject: score exactly 300 · PDC with score < 541 · Expat AECB overdue > 10,000.
  2. Current bucket > 0 → system stops the case. Scored: never Bucket 4 in last 12M. Non-scored: Bucket 2 max once (twice for Against TD); Bucket 3+ never in last 12M.
  3. Rescheduled: Expat 0–6 months ago → auto reject · > 12 months ago → auto pass · others → referred.
  4. Returned cheques/DDAs in 3 months (non-scored): STL Cat A/B/C, LS & Against TD up to 2 · others 1.
  5. Liability Settlement: facility being settled up to 29 DPD; overdue on any other facility paid first.
  6. UAE Nationals: government housing loan overdue ignored up to Bucket 3.

H. Allowed

  1. Top-up (LC): normally after 12 months; earlier if < 20x taken or income up by AED 200. Non-TML: earlier only after ≥ 3 instalments.
  2. Consolidation: PF & Auto Finance; credit card only with Head of Card approval.
  3. Liability Settlement: TML A, B, C, D + DIB staff (Cat D enabled by circular).
  4. LS on contactless journey allowed since circular – no signatures needed.
  5. Joint finance: STL TML only – husband/wife, parents, brothers/sisters, son/daughter.
  6. Long-tenure facility elsewhere: new PF can settle it within CB rules. Expats: income ≥ 10K, no deviation.
  7. Government housing loan > 300 months: fresh PF allowed unless ever on NDDSF list.

I. Not allowed

  1. Self-Employed – stopped since October 2022 (only by RCD deviation).
  2. Top-up for PDC Non-TML.
  3. LS for Non-TML – only once system is ready.
  4. Restricted: Bedouin/Stateless · "with Marsoom" · domestic workers on individual visa · FI outsource staff (all segments) · Non-TML outsource staff · bank's external auditors.
  5. DIB staff: no pre-approved offers for any product.
  6. PF as an overdraft.
  7. AECB shows NDDS → no further finance (NLS OK only if new PF closes it).
  8. Home Finance applicants: no PF for 2 months; PF never funds the down payment.
  9. Security cheque ≤ 120% of finance; not required for cash-secured.
  10. EMI date never on the 1st or 2nd.

J. Payment holiday (days to 1st EMI)

  1. DIB staff: 120.
  2. UAE National: DIB salary ≥ 25K 240 · DIB < 25K 180 · PDC 180.
  3. Expat: DIB ≥ 35K 180 · DIB < 35K 150 · PDC ≥ 35K 150 · PDC 10K–<35K 120 · PDC < 10K 90.

K. Finance Payment Relief

  1. Existing DIB customers, income dropped, DBR above CBUAE limit. Up to 89 DPD; Bucket 4+ → SAM.
  2. Salary within last 40 days · liability ≥ 10K · max age Expat 65, UAE National no cap.
  3. All PF + AF + cards → one Relief facility · tenure no cap · no extra cash · no fees.
  4. After approval: AECB "Arrangement" · ECL Stage 2 · blacklisted at CIF level · no new finance until 12 payments (or 3 if income improved + Relief settled).
  5. Not eligible: same income level · liabilities that can be consolidated normally · second rescheduling unless income dropped again.

L. Delegations

  1. Level 2 / Level 3: age +2 / +5 yrs · income 5% / 10% below · multiples +2 / +4 · current overdue Level 3 only, up to 500.
  2. CCO approves deviations up to AED 4M.
  3. Nothing beyond CBUAE limits – DBR, 20x/15x, 48/60 months.
