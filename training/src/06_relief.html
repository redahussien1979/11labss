POLICY SECTION 7.7

Finance Payment Relief Policy

THE BIG PICTURE
The Relief Policy helps an existing DIB customer who can no longer afford the instalments because income dropped (or, for self-employed, the business is under stress). All DIB finances are moved into one new contract with a longer tenure so that the monthly payment fits within the CBUAE DBR limit. No extra cash is given, there are no fees, and the account is reported to AECB as an "Arrangement".

7.7.1 – 7.7.4 Purpose

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 14
  • 7.7.1 Relief Program is for customers who have existing facility with DIB, are proactively seeking restructuring solution and currently in delinquency up to 89 DPD. Customers who are DELINQUENT (Bucket 4 & above) and looking for payment relief will be referred to SAM that has a separate remedial solution delegation for the management of delinquent accounts.
  • 7.7.2 The program seeks to help customers work out their payment situations with reduced monthly payment burden through payment period (prolonged / as per CB regulation) and without restructured amount. Please refer to "Fee & Charges" section.
  • 7.7.3 All relief measures are designed in alignment with applicable regulatory guidelines.
  • 7.7.4 Relief for customers will generally be for customers who fall in the below categories:

7.7.5 Salaried – who qualifies

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 14
  • 7.7.5.1 Reduction in monthly income due to any of the following reason and having financial difficulty to pay (Current overall DBR is > CBUAE permissible limits i.e. 30% for pensioners and 50% for non-pensioners). Drop of income also includes reduction in allowances such as OT / Incentive / hourly rate / housing allowance / HRA / fixed bonuses, customer status is changed from salaried to pensioner etc.
  • 7.7.5.2 Income reduction (Permanent / Temporary) with same employer.
  • 7.7.5.3 Customer lost his job, and salary with his new employer (Permanent / temporary) is lower (TML or NTML).
  • 7.7.5.4 Re-hired on temporary contract / hourly basis / reward salary without EOSB.

7.7.6 Self-Employed – who qualifies

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 14
  • 7.7.6.1 Self-Employed individuals under stress having business loss (negative cash flows, delayed payments etc.).
  • 7.7.6.2 Permanent disability / critical illness. (Customer should declare that along with all supporting documents leading to income reduction).
  • 7.7.6.3 The following will serve as guidelines to decide whether customer is "Stressed" or not: -
  • 7.7.6.4 Clearly highlighting the customer's stress situation through business proposal.
  • 7.7.6.5 Financial comparison and assessment of business or any other assessment showing that the customer is in stress from original finance and restructuring requests.
  • 7.7.6.6 Assessment of customer's financial position with other financers (Existing facilities status).
  • 7.7.6.7 Detailed field visit by qualified service provider may be done on case-to-case basis.
  • 7.7.6.8 Any other assessment as deemed necessary.

7.7.7 General Policy – Eligibility & Requirements

No.Policy text
7.7.7.1The Applicant must be currently employed / self-employed.
7.7.7.2For salaried individuals, Relief Policy to be offered after ensuring at least one salary (from existing or new employer) is credited in the bank account in last 40 days.
7.7.7.3Both DIB and Non-DIB account holders shall be allowed, including employees of DIB and its subsidiaries tagged to DIB group in TML list. Income and liability assessment of DIB staff cases will be done in coordination with HR.
7.7.7.4Current financial status of the application shall be regular or up to 89 DPD.
7.7.7.5The Month on Book (MOB) for any finance shall be set to 3 months without considering the number of paid installments. The MOB can be set to 1 month upon approval from the Head of Retail Credit (HORC).
7.7.7.6Maximum age at maturity 65 for Expat and no cap for UAE Nationals.
7.7.7.7Minimum liability requirement for Relief Policy solution, AED 10,000.
7.7.7.8No extra cash pay-out shall be allowed.
7.7.7.9Tenure: No cap. To be aligned accordingly to bring the DBR up to CBUAE DBR permissible limits (including internal / external liabilities in bank statement / AECB).
7.7.7.10Minimum reduced income shall be up to AED 1,000 with 10% acceptable approval from HORC.
7.7.7.11First installment on the Relief Policy contract shall be collected / recovered on the next salary date (within 30 days). The first installment date can be up to 90 days upon approval from the HORC.
7.7.7.12Regular installment postponement shall be allowed after paying 6 regular EMIs.
7.7.7.13For PDC, if bank wide liabilities are within the prevailing eligibility / CB regulations, utmost efforts shall be made to buy out customer liabilities / acquire salary. This is only for PF TML employers.
7.7.7.14Outstanding balance of all finances with DIB (Personal Finance, Auto Finance and Covered card) to be transferred to the Relief Policy contract for separate tracking / monitoring purpose.
7.7.7.15Customer transferred their new salary from the new employer to other bank (old being with DIB) should transfer salary back to DIB or have arrangements with new salaried bank to buy out DIB liabilities.
7.7.7.16For Noor Bank migrated customers, they may not meet the condition of salary reduction due to different income assessment hence DIB income assessment policy will be applied.
7.7.7.17Contact point verifications, as per prevailing policy.
7.7.7.18AECB Check is mandatory for all type of customers.
7.7.7.19For Non TML employers, due diligence as per existing parameters.
7.7.7.20Card can be retained with lower or same limit post completing retention process.
7.7.7.21Accounts approved under Relief Policy are tagged separately in Asset Originating System / core banking for tracking and reporting purpose.
7.7.7.22All type of restrictions is waived. These restrictions are, typically, not limited to industry restriction, company's operating area, employer restriction, income / LOS / or any other restriction on employer, nationality, minimum income etc.
7.7.7.23Upon Approval, customer details will be classified / updated:
7.7.7.23.1 Blacklist in core banking, at CIF Level.
7.7.7.23.2 Contract to be classified as "Arrangement" in AECB to restrict additional finance from market.
TRAINER NOTE
7.7.7.10 ("Minimum reduced income shall be up to AED 1,000 with 10% acceptable approval from HORC") is not clearly worded in the source. Ask RCPPM how it is applied before using it in decisions.

7.7.8 Treatment of other Finances

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 15
  • 7.7.8.1 All existing finances within (Personal Finance, Auto Finance and Covered card) must be settled under Relief Policy, bring the DBR within CBUAE prescribed limits however tenure can be extended beyond CBUAE prescribed tenure limits
  • 7.7.8.2 Treatment of cash secured Finances: Cash secured facility must be closed, and residual collateral should be used to pay other facility to avoid Relief Policy. If the DBR is still more than CBUAE DBR threshold then Relief Policy request can be accepted. Relief Policy can be allowed without liquidating cash secured collateral upon approval from HORC.

7.7.9 Exclusion

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 15
  • 7.7.9.1 If liabilities can be consolidated within CBUAE regulation (no tenure reduction or discount on profit) with or without additional cash.
  • 7.7.9.2 If provisioned accounts or current delinquency level is bucket 4 & above.
  • 7.7.9.3 If customer does not have any source of income. Exceptionally rental income can be considered for no-job cases.
  • 7.7.9.4 Stressed customers with same income level are not eligible.
  • 7.7.9.5 Second rescheduling on already rescheduled account is not allowed unless there is further reduction in income and DBR on reduced income is > CBUAE DBR thresholds.
  • 7.7.9.6 DIB internal blacklist customers will not be eligible. However, if they are blacklisted due to facility that is being consolidated under Relief Policy, they can be accepted. Acceptance of any other blacklisting scenarios is at RCD discretion. subject to obtaining clearance from unit that blacklisted the customer or employer

7.7.10 – 7.7.14 Instalment, future finance, settlement, fees, AECB

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 15
  • 7.7.10 Monthly Instalment Payment
    7.7.10.1 For salaried / pensioners applicants, new monthly instalment payment plus monthly payments of all outside and inside debt must not be more than CBUAE DBR permissible limits of the monthly income for salaried customers and 30% for pensioners.
  • 7.7.11 Future Finance or Settlement
    7.7.11.1 The Relief Policy account can be settled at a later date as per settlement policy / process.
    7.7.11.2 No new Finances / other products will be approved before 12 regular payments of restructured finance.
    7.7.11.3 If customer income has improved e.g. changed employer and got a better package, then new finance can be considered after paying 3 instalments. In such scenario, Relief Policy contract must be settled by the customer.
    7.7.11.4 New finance shall be processed as per existing policy's eligibility parameters and within CBUAE norms.
  • 7.7.12 Settlement of Existing Finance (s)
    7.7.12.1 Existing finance(s) contractual outstanding (principal + accrued profit) will be settled with via a fresh contract with no additional cash. The settlement to be paid by customer in instalments. Please refer to Annexure 1 on the process of settlement
  • 7.7.13 Fees & Charges
    7.7.13.1 No fee or charges for Due Diligence and Documentation for Finance Payment Relief Policy.
  • 7.7.14 AECB Reporting and Staging
    7.7.14.1 Relief Policy accounts will be reported as "Arrangement" in AECB.
    7.7.14.2 Relief Policy accounts will be reported under Stage 2 for ECL computation.
TRAINER NOTE
7.7.12.1 refers to "Annexure 1" for the settlement process; in this document that process is Annexure 8.4 "Process on Settlement of Existing Finance (S)".

7.7.15 – 7.7.17 Documents and Income Proof

POLICY TEXTPolicy pages 15–16
  • 7.7.15 Documents
    • 7.7.15.1 Valid emirates ID copy, valid passport copy and valid visa page copy from expatriates. If sponsor information is available on Emirates ID then visa page from passport is not required.
    • 7.7.15.2 Fresh AECB report will be obtained. AECB consent is not required as customer already has finance with DIB.
    • 7.7.15.3 Customer's written request expressing difficulty in repaying debt outstanding as a result of a proven status of severe financial distress & willing to fulfil his commitments if solution is provided.
    • 7.7.15.4 Resignation / termination letter from previous employer if salaried.
    • 7.7.15.5 Rehiring letter from employer if salaried.
    • 7.7.15.6 If long term disability / Illness: Confirmation letter from a doctor / hospital.
    • 7.7.15.7 Security cheque. One security cheque equivalent to 100% of the finance amount to be obtained for Ijara and equivalent to debit balance (i.e. Total principal and profit) for Murabaha.
    • 7.7.15.8 To restructure the facilities and issue new installment plans to the customer, the relevant provisions of the underlying contracts between the Bank and the customer shall be amended and exchanged to capture express consent of the parties.
  • 7.7.16 Income Proof
    • 7.7.16.1 For Salaried individuals
    • 7.7.16.2 Latest 3 months bank statement to analyze the current salary, liabilities & turnover.
    • 7.7.16.3 For Unemployed individuals, layoff letter, accepted resignation letter and new employment offer.
    • 7.7.16.4 Current salary: salary stubs, employer's letter – valid at the time of submission.
    • 7.7.16.5 Any other income such as Rental income etc. can also be considered.
  • 7.7.17 For Self-Employed customers
    • 7.7.17.1 Valid income generating source i.e. present Bank statement, net cash flow statement or any other source of fund.
    • 7.7.17.2 Company's latest constitutional documents to ensure no change in organization structure.
    • 7.7.17.3 Provide information of cash flow and net worth which include deposit, investment and properties.
    • 7.7.17.4 Provide all outside debt and monthly payment.
    • 7.7.17.5 Bank statement and / or audited financials.
    • 7.7.17.6 Business recommendation with proposal which should cover following minimum requirements: -
      • 7.7.17.6.1 Details of company and owner.
      • 7.7.17.6.2 Current finances details.
      • 7.7.17.6.3 Confirmation of meeting with customer to assess customer's stress situation and business.
      • 7.7.17.6.4 Fresh field visit.
      • 7.7.17.6.5 A new set of security and payment instruments (DDA, SI etc.).

WORKED EXAMPLE 1 – SALARIED CUSTOMER WHO QUALIFIES
Customer AJ (Expat, age 45), salary was AED 20,000 → cut to AED 14,000 by the same employer (7.7.5.2) Current status: 30 DPD (≤ 89 DPD ✔) Latest salary credited 12 days ago (≤ 40 days ✔) DIB obligations: PF EMI 6,500 + AF EMI 2,500 = 9,000; outside debt (AECB): 1,000 Current DBR = (9,000 + 1,000) ÷ 14,000 = 71.4% > 50% → financially stressed ✔ Total DIB outstanding: PF 180,000 + AF 60,000 + Covered card 15,000 = AED 255,000 (≥ 10,000 ✔) Relief solution Max total obligations = 50% × 14,000 = AED 7,000 Max new Relief EMI = 7,000 − 1,000 (outside debt) = AED 6,000 All DIB balances (PF + AF + card) → ONE Relief contract (7.7.7.14 / 7.7.8.1) Tenure extended (no cap) until EMI ≤ 6,000, but age at maturity ≤ 65 (Expat) No extra cash; no fees; first instalment on next salary date (within 30 days)
After approval: blacklisted at CIF level in core banking; reported as "Arrangement" in AECB; Stage 2 for ECL. The card may be retained with a lower or the same limit after the retention process.
