Part A

Definitions, Purpose & Scope (Sections 1–4)

Q1MULTIPLE CHOICEPolicy: Sec. 4
A customer holds an Omani passport and works in Dubai. For this policy the customer is:
  1. UAE National
  2. Expat
  3. GCC resident – treated as UAE National
  4. Non-resident
Answer: B – GCC nationals are categorised as Expat (definition of EXPAT).
Q2MULTIPLE CHOICEPolicy: Sec. 4
Which statement about DIB Staff is correct?
  1. They are processed like Non-TML customers
  2. They can be offered pre-approved PF if tagged in Core
  3. They are ineligible for financing under the pre-approved segment for any product
  4. Their DBR is calculated by RCD
Answer: C – DIB staff are ineligible to receive financing under the pre-approved segment for any product; their income/liability data is restricted.
Q3MULTIPLE CHOICEPolicy: Sec. 4
An application fails one policy-engine parameter and the deviation is minor as per the delegation matrix. It is a:
  1. Level 1 case
  2. Level 2 case
  3. Level 3 case
  4. Policy-exception (CCO) case
Answer: B – Level 2 = minor deviation; Level 1 = no deviation; Level 3 = more severe than Level 2.
Q4MULTIPLE CHOICEPolicy: Sec. 3
Which of the following is NOT covered by this Policy?
  1. Repeat finance deals underwritten by RCD
  2. Applications submitted via digital channels
  3. Fresh finance for salaried customers with verifiable income
  4. Underwriting parameters for stressed customers
Answer: D – The policy does not cover underwriting parameters for stressed customers.

Q5MULTIPLE CHOICEPolicy: Sec. 5 – STL
Which employer categories qualify for the STL segment (salary with DIB + STL in DIB format)?
  1. A and B only
  2. A, B and C
  3. A, B, C and D
  4. Any TML or Non-TML employer
Answer: B – TML category A, B & C (plus DIB staff treated as STL Cat A, Wajaha salaried and Ayaan ≥ 12 months).
Q6CASE / SHORT ANSWERPolicy: Sec. 5 – STL waiver
Customer works for a TML Cat B employer, has provided STL in DIB format, but no salary has been credited to DIB yet. HR-CPV confirms next salary will be credited to DIB. Can the "first salary in DIB" requirement be waived?
Answer: Yes – criterion (a) Confirmed Future Credit. Waivers apply only to Cat A, B & C with STL provided.
Q7CALCULATIONPolicy: Sec. 5 – STL waiver (d)
TML Cat A customer changed job and does not have 3 salary credits in the last 90 days. Last salary from old employer: 15 February; latest salary from new employer: 10 June (non-leap year). Can the case be accepted under the New-to-Country policy?
Answer:
15 Feb → 28 Feb = 13 days March 31 + April 30 + May 31 = 92 days 1 Jun → 10 Jun = 10 days Gap = 13 + 92 + 10 = 115 days ≤ 121 days
Yes – the gap is within 121 days.
Q8CALCULATIONPolicy: Sec. 5 – STL waiver (d)
Same rule as previous question. Last salary old employer: 20 January; latest salary new employer: 25 May (non-leap year). Accepted?
Answer:
20 Jan → 31 Jan = 11 days Feb 28 + Mar 31 + Apr 30 = 89 days 1 May → 25 May = 25 days Gap = 11 + 89 + 25 = 125 days > 121 days
No – gap exceeds 121 days, so waiver (d) does not apply.
Q9CASE / SHORT ANSWERPolicy: Sec. 5 – Salaried/Privileged
Non-TML employee. Salary credits: March – DIB, April – Bank X, May – Bank X. Does the customer meet the Salaried/Privileged salary-transfer requirement?
Answer: No – 3 salaries in total are present, but at least 1 in the most recent month must be credited to DIB. May (latest) went to Bank X.

Q25CASE / SHORT ANSWERPolicy: Note D-I
Which of these is an AECB auto-reject? (a) Local, STL, score 300 (b) Expat, STL, score 610, overdue AED 11,000 (c) Expat, PDC, score 535 (d) Local, STL, score 600, overdue AED 9,000

Q27CASE / SHORT ANSWERPolicy: Note D-II

Answer: Bank X: overdue up to 29 DPD is allowed on facilities being settled → 35 DPD is not acceptable.

Q28CASE / SHORT ANSWERPolicy: Note D-V
UAE National, salary transfer segment, Government Housing Program loan overdue at 75 DPD (Bucket 3). Decision on that overdue?
Answer: Can be disregarded – up to Bucket 3 (< 90 DPD). Bucket 4+ must be settled and updated in AECB before approval. Applies only to Liability segment and salary transfer segment.
Q29CASE / SHORT ANSWERPolicy: Note D-I / D-IV

Answer: Rule I says Expats and UAE Nationals with score < 541 in PDC → Auto Reject; Rule IV says Local under PDC with score < 541 → Referred.
⚠ NOT 100% SURE: The two rules conflict for UAE Nationals. Confirm the system behaviour with RCPPM.

Q39CALCULATIONPolicy: 6.4
