POLICY SECTIONS 1 – 3

Introduction, Purpose and Scope

1. Introduction

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 1
This Personal Finance Operating Credit Policy (hereafter referred to as this "Policy") establishes the comprehensive credit principles, eligibility criteria, approval standards, and risk controls governing the origination, assessment, approval and management of Personal Finance facilities offered by the Bank to individual customers. This Policy ensures that personal finance is extended in a prudent, responsible, Sharia-compliant, and regulatorily compliant manner, in alignment with:
  • Bank's approved Group Risk Management Framework
  • CBUAE Regulations and notices
  • Consumer Protection regulations and standards
  • Sharia principles as approved by the ISSC
This Policy serves as the authoritative reference document for all stakeholders involved in the Personal Finance lifecycle, including Front-End Sales, Credit Underwriting, Risk, Operations, Compliance, Audit, and Regulators.
IN SIMPLE WORDS
This is the "rule book" for Personal Finance (PF). It tells everyone – Sales, Credit, Risk, Operations, Compliance, Audit – who can get PF, how much, on what terms and who may approve exceptions. Every PF must respect four things at the same time: the Bank's risk framework, Central Bank (CBUAE) rules, consumer-protection rules and Sharia rules approved by the Internal Sharia Supervision Committee (ISSC).

2. Purpose

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 1
The purpose of this Policy is to:
  • Consolidate and formalize all previously approved Personal Finance credit parameters;
  • Define minimum eligibility standards, acceptable risk profiles, and financing limits;
  • Establish clear rules for fresh finance, liability consolidation, liability settlement, and special schemes;
  • Ensure consistent credit decision-making across all Personal Finance segments;
  • Mitigate credit, operational, regulatory, consumer, and Sharia risk.
IN SIMPLE WORDS
All older PF approvals and memos are now brought together in one place, so that two analysts looking at the same case reach the same decision.

3. Scope

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 1
This Policy outlines the following scope:
  • This is an operational Policy and serves as a comprehensive guideline for the Credit Approval Team and all end users, specifying Personal Finance eligibility criteria in alignment with Dubai Islamic Bank's (hereafter referred to as the 'Bank' or 'DIB') risk appetite and regulatory requirements.
  • This Policy applies to all segments and sub-segments within Personal Finance for individual and select self-employed individual customers who have a regular and verifiable income stream.
  • This Policy applies to all fresh, new and repeat finance deals underwritten by the Retail Credit Department and applications submitted via digital channels. This Policy does not cover underwriting parameters for stressed customers.
  • This Policy is governed by Bank's approved Policy and Process Management Framework and shall be reviewed and renewed in accordance with the framework.
  • The Personal Finance Product is offered under its Product Development Document (PDD) and the structure and risks are duly captured therein.
IN SIMPLE WORDS
  • Covered: individual customers (and selected self-employed) with regular, verifiable income; fresh, new and repeat deals; branch and digital applications.
  • Not covered here: underwriting parameters for stressed customers (these are handled separately – see the Finance Payment Relief section and SAM).
  • The product itself (structure, Sharia contract, risks) is described in the PDD; this Policy describes the credit rules.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Customer A is a salaried expat with a steady monthly salary credit → in scope.
Customer B has no regular income and asks for a new PF → not eligible under this policy (no regular, verifiable income stream).
Customer C applied through the DIB mobile app → in scope (digital channels are covered).

IN SIMPLE WORDS – THE TERMS YOU WILL USE DAILY
  • TML = the bank's list of enlisted employers (maintained under the TML Enlistment Policy), grouped into categories A, B, C, D. An employer that is not enlisted is Non-TML (NTM/NTML). (The policy does not spell out the letters "TML" – use the term as is.)
  • STL = Salary Transfer Letter: the employer's letter confirming the salary will be paid into DIB.
  • PDC = customers whose salary goes to another bank (the name comes from the old practice of financing against post-dated cheques).
  • Expat = everyone who is not a UAE National. GCC nationals are treated as Expats.
  • Level 1 / 2 / 3 = how far an application is from policy: Level 1 = no deviation; Level 2 = minor deviation; Level 3 = more severe deviation (see Annexure 8.2 for the exact limits and approvers).
  • DIB Staff = employees of DIB and subsidiaries tagged as staff in the Core system. Their income/liability data is restricted, and they cannot get pre-approved offers for any product.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A Saudi national working in Dubai applies for PF. For this policy the customer is an Expat (GCC nationals are categorised as Expat), so all Expat limits apply – for example the Expat maximum age at maturity and the Expat maximum finance tables.
WATCH OUT
A DIB staff member must never be processed under a pre-approved segment, even if the system shows them a pre-approved limit.
