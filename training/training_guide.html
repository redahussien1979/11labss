POLICY SECTIONS 1 – 3

Introduction, Purpose and Scope

1. Introduction

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 1
This Personal Finance Operating Credit Policy (hereafter referred to as this "Policy") establishes the comprehensive credit principles, eligibility criteria, approval standards, and risk controls governing the origination, assessment, approval and management of Personal Finance facilities offered by the Bank to individual customers. This Policy ensures that personal finance is extended in a prudent, responsible, Sharia-compliant, and regulatorily compliant manner, in alignment with:
  • Bank's approved Group Risk Management Framework
  • CBUAE Regulations and notices
  • Consumer Protection regulations and standards
  • Sharia principles as approved by the ISSC
This Policy serves as the authoritative reference document for all stakeholders involved in the Personal Finance lifecycle, including Front-End Sales, Credit Underwriting, Risk, Operations, Compliance, Audit, and Regulators.
IN SIMPLE WORDS
This is the "rule book" for Personal Finance (PF). It tells everyone – Sales, Credit, Risk, Operations, Compliance, Audit – who can get PF, how much, on what terms and who may approve exceptions. Every PF must respect four things at the same time: the Bank's risk framework, Central Bank (CBUAE) rules, consumer-protection rules and Sharia rules approved by the Internal Sharia Supervision Committee (ISSC).

2. Purpose

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 1
The purpose of this Policy is to:
  • Consolidate and formalize all previously approved Personal Finance credit parameters;
  • Define minimum eligibility standards, acceptable risk profiles, and financing limits;
  • Establish clear rules for fresh finance, liability consolidation, liability settlement, and special schemes;
  • Ensure consistent credit decision-making across all Personal Finance segments;
  • Mitigate credit, operational, regulatory, consumer, and Sharia risk.
IN SIMPLE WORDS
All older PF approvals and memos are now brought together in one place, so that two analysts looking at the same case reach the same decision.

3. Scope

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 1
This Policy outlines the following scope:
  • This is an operational Policy and serves as a comprehensive guideline for the Credit Approval Team and all end users, specifying Personal Finance eligibility criteria in alignment with Dubai Islamic Bank's (hereafter referred to as the 'Bank' or 'DIB') risk appetite and regulatory requirements.
  • This Policy applies to all segments and sub-segments within Personal Finance for individual and select self-employed individual customers who have a regular and verifiable income stream.
  • This Policy applies to all fresh, new and repeat finance deals underwritten by the Retail Credit Department and applications submitted via digital channels. This Policy does not cover underwriting parameters for stressed customers.
  • This Policy is governed by Bank's approved Policy and Process Management Framework and shall be reviewed and renewed in accordance with the framework.
  • The Personal Finance Product is offered under its Product Development Document (PDD) and the structure and risks are duly captured therein.
IN SIMPLE WORDS
  • Covered: individual customers (and selected self-employed) with regular, verifiable income; fresh, new and repeat deals; branch and digital applications.
  • Not covered here: underwriting parameters for stressed customers (these are handled separately – see the Finance Payment Relief section and SAM).
  • The product itself (structure, Sharia contract, risks) is described in the PDD; this Policy describes the credit rules.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Customer A is a salaried expat with a steady monthly salary credit → in scope.
Customer B has no regular income and asks for a new PF → not eligible under this policy (no regular, verifiable income stream).
Customer C applied through the DIB mobile app → in scope (digital channels are covered).

IN SIMPLE WORDS
  • STL is the best segment: salary comes to DIB, the employer is TML A, B or C, and the employer has signed an STL in DIB format.
  • Some employers (e.g. Al Futtaim group, Emirates Airlines, employers with special instructions in Finnone, and Government/semi-government entities) do not have to use the DIB STL format.
  • DIB staff are treated like STL TML Cat A.
  • Wajaha (salaried) and Ayaan (relationship ≥ 12 months) customers also fall here. If their case comes through the normal retail channel → normal STL rules. If it comes through their own unit RM → Private Banking rules (section 7.6).
  • Normal requirement: 3 salary transfers, and the latest month must be in DIB.
  • Waiver of "first salary in DIB" (Cat A/B/C only, STL required) if any one of a)–e) is met.
WORKED EXAMPLE 1 – FIRST SALARY WAIVER
Customer A works for a TML Cat B company, has just opened a DIB account and has provided an STL in DIB format. No salary has reached DIB yet. HR-CPV confirms that next month's salary will be credited to DIB.
→ Criterion a) Confirmed Future Credit is met → the "first salary in DIB" requirement can be waived.
WORKED EXAMPLE 2 – JOB CHANGE (CRITERION d)
Customer B (TML Cat A, STL provided) changed job. Salary credits in the last 90 days: only one (new employer). Last salary from old employer: 10 January. Latest salary from new employer: 5 May.
Gap = 10 Jan → 5 May = 115 days (≤ 121 days)
→ Can be accepted under the New-to-Country policy. If the gap had been, say, 130 days → not covered by this waiver.
WORKED EXAMPLE 3 – CATEGORY D
Customer C works for a TML Cat D employer and has no salary in DIB yet. → The waivers do not apply (only A, B, C). At least one salary must already be credited to DIB. Also note that Cat D falls in the Salaried/Privileged segment, not STL (see 5-B).

POLICY SECTION 5 – REMARKS

5.1 – 5.4 Schemes explained

5.1 Liability Consolidation – LC (Top-up)

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 4
  1. 5.1.1. Only PF & AF products are allowed for consolidation, CC consolidation is subject to Head of Card approval
  2. 5.1.2. Customer should have at least 1 PF for a period of 12 months (from booking date)
  3. 5.1.3. If a customer did not avail 20 multiples in previous finance or there is an increase in his/her income, at least AED 200, in the subsequent months due to promotion, any new cash allowance added to income, any allowance which was not considered in previous finance, lowest of last 3 salary was considered in previous deal, discontinuation of any regular income deduction or deduction of liability that results in increase in [in]come or DBR room etc. Incremental finance shall be allowed under Liability Consolidation before completing 12 months period. For Non-TML companies, applicant should pay [at] least 3 installments to qualify for Liability Consolidation before 12 months
  4. 5.1.4. Liability Consolidation workflow will be enabled to allow Personal Finance under Liability Consolidation scheme when 12 months period condition is not met (subject to system implementation). This will allow to retain AF-Only customers who are moving salary transfer relationship to another bank. By enabling this workflow, the DSR calculation and settlement of Auto Finance liability will be automated. This is subject to customer meeting Personal finance policy norms.
IN SIMPLE WORDS
  • What it is: the existing DIB PF (and/or AF) is closed into a new, bigger PF – the customer gets extra ("incremental") cash.
  • What can be consolidated: PF and AF. Credit Card only with Head of Card approval.
  • Normal rule: the existing PF must be at least 12 months old from booking date.
  • Before 12 months it is allowed if there is "room": the customer did not take the full 20x last time, or income increased by at least AED 200 (promotion, new cash allowance, allowance not counted before, the lowest of 3 salaries was used last time, a regular deduction or liability stopped, etc.).
  • Non-TML employer: before 12 months the customer must have paid at least 3 instalments.
  • 5.1.4 is a planned system workflow (subject to implementation) to retain AF-only customers who move their salary to another bank.
WORKED EXAMPLE 1 – BEFORE 12 MONTHS, MULTIPLES ROOM
Customer E (TML Cat A, STL) took a PF 7 months ago at 15x salary. Salary unchanged.
→ Previous finance did not use 20 multiples → incremental finance under LC is allowed before 12 months (subject to all other norms: DBR, max finance, etc.).
WORKED EXAMPLE 2 – INCOME INCREASE
Customer F took 20x six months ago on salary AED 12,000. After a promotion the salary is AED 12,150.
Increase = 12,150 − 12,000 = AED 150 → less than AED 200
→ Income increase is not enough on its own. If the new salary were AED 12,300 (increase AED 300 ≥ 200) → incremental finance allowed before 12 months.
WORKED EXAMPLE 3 – NON-TML
Customer G works for a Non-TML company (Salaried/Privileged), took 15x four months ago and has paid 2 instalments. → Not yet – needs at least 3 instalments paid. After the 3rd instalment → can qualify.

5.2 Liability Settlement (LS)

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 4
  1. 5.2.1. Eligibility: Allowed for both DIB existing salary transfer customers and Non-DIB salary transfer customers wishing to transfer liabilities and salary transfer relationship to DIB
  2. 5.2.2. Permissible TML categories: TML A, B, C, D, and DIB Staff. Non TML is approved and can be implemented once system readiness is confirmed
  3. 5.2.3. Additional Facilities: Customers may apply for Personal Finance (PF) or Auto Finance (AF) once the first salary is credited to their DIB account.
  4. 5.2.4. Covered Cards: A covered card can be offered upon submission of a Salary Transfer Letter (STL) and Clearance Letters from all banks where liabilities were settled
IN SIMPLE WORDS
DIB pays off ("buys out") the customer's loans at other banks and the customer moves the salary to DIB. Allowed for TML A–D and DIB staff. Non-TML is approved but waits for system readiness. A further PF/AF can be applied for only after the first salary arrives in DIB. A covered card needs the STL plus clearance letters from all banks that were settled.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Customer H (TML Cat C) has a PF at Bank X and a car loan at Bank Y. DIB settles both and the salary moves to DIB.
• Wants an additional AF the following week, before any salary reached DIB → wait until the first salary is credited.
• Wants a covered card → needs STL + clearance letters from both Bank X and Bank Y.

6.4 Debt Burden Ratio (DBR) CBUAE

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 6
  1. The DBR is calculated individually for each income type which is 30% is applied to the pension income, and 50% is applied to the salary income
  2. The DBR calculation for DIB staff is managed exclusively by DIB-HR. This ensures that staff DBR assessments are handled according to internal policies and process.
  3. When a customer has dual income streams (pension and salary), calculating DBR as 30% of pension and 50% of salary can be challenging. POLICY SECTION 7.7

Finance Payment Relief Policy

THE BIG PICTURE
The Relief Policy helps an existing DIB customer who can no longer afford the instalments because income dropped (or, for self-employed, the business is under stress). All DIB finances are moved into one new contract with a longer tenure so that the monthly payment fits within the CBUAE DBR limit. No extra cash is given, there are no fees, and the account is reported to AECB as an "Arrangement".

7.7.1 – 7.7.4 Purpose

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 14
  • 7.7.1 Relief Program is for customers who have existing facility with DIB, are proactively seeking restructuring solution and currently in delinquency up to 89 DPD. Customers who are DELINQUENT (Bucket 4 & above) and looking for payment relief will be referred to SAM that has a separate remedial solution delegation for the management of delinquent accounts.
  • 7.7.2 The program seeks to help customers work out their payment situations with reduced monthly payment burden through payment period (prolonged / as per CB regulation) and without restructured amount. Please refer to "Fee & Charges" section.
  • 7.7.3 All relief measures are designed in alignment with applicable regulatory guidelines.
  • 7.7.4 Relief for customers will generally be for customers who fall in the below categories:

7.7.5 Salaried – who qualifies

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 14

Drop of income also includes reduction in allowances such as OT / Incentive / hourly rate / housing allowance / HRA / fixed bonuses, customer status is changed from salaried to pensioner etc.
  • 7.7.5.2 Income reduction (Permanent / Temporary) with same employer.
  • 7.7.5.3 Customer lost his job, and salary with his new employer (Permanent / temporary) is lower (TML or NTML).

POLICY SECTION 8.4

Process on Settlement of Existing Finance (S) – Relief

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 22
  1. DIB front-end staff will verify the following details from the customer to ascertain customers pre-qualification under relief policy:
    1. Customer must have an existing facility with DIB.
    2. Customers income is impacted as clarified in point 7.7.5 or 7.7.6 from the time the original facility was applied; and
    3. The total obligations of the customer are more than fifty percent of customer's revised salary.
    Once the above conditions are met, as per Central Bank regulations, customer must be provided relief so that his monthly obligations are brough below fifty percent of the revised salary.
  2. The bank will ask the customer the required documents (as applicable) and in addition will seek a customer request / application to be treated under the relief policy.
  3. Based on the documentation shared and proof of financial stress the retail credit department will analyze the case. As part of their checks:
    1. Fresh AECB report is pulled to analyze the complete customer financial obligations within DIB and outside DIB.
    2. Other checks as per the RCD policy are done.
  4. If the customer meets the "distressed" criteria (as per Central Bank Mandatory requirement: Monthly payment is more than CBUAE DBR permissible limits):
    1. Customer will be classified as distressed and considered approved under the Relief Policy.
    2. The duration of the facility will be extended such that customer monthly payment is maximized to CBUAE permissible DBR limits of customer income.
    3. Customer will be asked to provide consent to settle all their existing facilities into 1 facility i.e. the Relief facility. So, if customer has multiple Covered Cards, Personal finance and / or Auto finance they will be closed and the new Relief Facility will be created.
    4. Finance Payment Relief offer extended to the customer will be subject to a fresh and new credit assessment.
  5. Once the case is approved, DIB will be following steps, based on standard personal finance approval terms
    1. National Bonds already purchased and owned by DIB equivalent to the amount approved (the relief facility) will be sold to the customer at a price which will be based on cost and profit amount. Cost will be equal to the relief facility amount required.
    2. National Bond will receive customer details and the deal information and send a confirmation to the Bank of the transfer of the title to the customer.
    3. Simultaneously, NBC will send a message to the customer confirming the transfer of the title of the Sukuk to him.
    4. Customer may agree to redeem them via electronic communication between National Bond and the customer.
    5. Subsequently, once redemption is requested by the customer NBC will instruct DIB to transfer funds from their account with DIB to the customer's account.
    6. Based on this request, DIB will transfer funds in customer account.
  6. This amount received under the Relief facility will be used to settle all the customer's existing facilities.
    1. Funds will be credited in the customer account by DIB based on the instructions received from National Bonds from their account with DIB.
      1. The customer account already exists in which this facility will be disbursed.
      2. No hold or block is placed on the customer account
    2. From the proceeds in the Customer's account existing liabilities are settled except Home Finance proceeds (which is dealt with in accordance with sub-clause iv below).
      1. In case if customer has multiple liabilities (E.g. Covered cards, Personal Finance, Auto Finance etc.) the liabilities are settled one by one.
      2. Any unrealized profits on existing facilities being settled i.e. Personal Finance and Auto Finance (where applicable) shall be waived off i.e. rebate provided to the customer. (This will not be applicable to cards)
      3. This new facility amount will approve in a manner which will not result in any additional liquidity to the customer other than the amount for settlement of existing facilities and will always be less than the combined outstanding liability of the Customer. This is in light of the AAOIFI Sharia Standard 59 – Clause 4.1.3.3.
      4. Home Finance facility will not be settled by Commodity Murabaha proceeds. Instead, the Bank may reduce future periods' profit rates or sell the asset to the Customer at a discounted price.
    3. Once the above steps are completed, Customer will only have one facility with DIB which is the Relief facility.
      1. Customer will have a regular monthly payment for this new facility which will be almost equal to CBUAE DBR threshold of their income.
      2. The payment will be fixed for the duration of the new facility.
      3. No new Finances / other products will be approved until the Finance Payment relief linked contract is active.
      4. If customer income has improved i.e. changed employer and have got better package, then new finance can be considered subject to the full settlement of the Finance Payment relief facility.
Note: This annexure outlines the process as it exists at the time of this Policy's issuance. In the event of any changes to the original or primary process, the updated process will take precedence over the content of this annexure without the need for a policy revision.

① Front-end pre-qualifies: existing DIB facility + income impacted (7.7.5/7.7.6) + obligations > 50% of revised salary ② Customer gives documents + written request for Relief ③ RCD analyses: fresh AECB (inside & outside DIB) + other RCD checks ④ "Distressed" (payment > CBUAE DBR limit) → approved; tenure extended; consent to merge all facilities into 1 ⑤ Sharia structure: DIB sells National Bonds Sukuk (cost + profit) to customer → NBC confirms title → customer redeems electronically → NBC instructs DIB → DIB credits customer account (no hold/block) ⑥ Proceeds settle each facility one by one (PF/AF unrealised profit rebated; not for cards; HF not settled with these proceeds) → customer is left with ONE Relief facility, fixed EMI ≈ DBR limit

Quiz – 20 questions with answers

Q1. A Kuwaiti national applies for PF. Is the customer treated as UAE National or Expat?
Expat – GCC nationals are categorised as Expat (section 4).
Q2. A self-employed doctor applies for a normal PF. What is the decision?
Not eligible – Self-Employed segment stopped in October 2022 (only RCD deviation using Annexure 8.3).
Q3. TML Cat B customer, STL in DIB format, no salary in DIB yet, salary certificate verified via a government portal. Can the first-salary requirement be waived?
Yes – criterion b) STL/SC Validation (A/B/C only).
Q4. PDC customer working for a Non-TML company asks for Liability Consolidation. Allowed?
No – "Liability Consolidation for Non TML under PDC is not allowed".
Q5. UAE National, 20 years old, DIB salary AED 15,000. Maximum finance?
Lower of 10 × 15,000 = 150,000 or 250,000 → AED 150,000 (STL only, PDC not allowed).
Q6. Expat, PDC, AECB score 530. Result?
Auto reject – PDC with score < 541.
Q7. Salary AED 18,000, existing obligations AED 4,000. Maximum new monthly instalment under DBR?
50% × 18,000 = 9,000 − 4,000 = AED 5,000.
Q8. MOD employee, salary AED 20,000. Maximum multiples and tenure?
15x (AED 300,000) and 60 months.
Q9. Expat, Medium risk, salary AED 6,500, wants 15x. Decision?
Band ≥5K–<8K allows 14x → 15x is more than specified → auto-reject (Note 2). A +2 multiples deviation (Level 2) would be needed.
Q10. Expat, DIB salary AED 40,000. Maximum payment holiday for the first EMI?
180 days (DIB salary incl. LS, income ≥ 35K).
Q11. Finance AED 300,000. Maximum security cheque value?
120% × 300,000 = AED 360,000.
Q12. Salaried customer with annual income AED 50,000 blocks a TD of AED 80,000. Maximum PF (principal + profit)?
Annual income ≤ 60K → 50% → AED 40,000 including profit.
Q13. Car price AED 120,000, PDC TML customer. Maximum Sukuk Pack?
20% × 120,000 = AED 24,000 (within AED 200,000 exposure cap).
Q14. Home Finance Sukuk Pack: should PF Sales pull a new AECB report?
No – AECB is already pulled by the HF team (7.4.5 / 7.4.8.1).
Q15. A customer applies for a Covered Card and is a pensioner. Will Finnone generate a cross-sell PF limit?
No – pensioners are excluded (7.5, 4.7).
Q16. Ayaan customer with 10 months' relationship. Eligible for Private Banking PF?
Policy minimum is 12 months → not eligible at Level 1; 2 months short is within the Level 2 deviation (3Ms below policy).
Q17. Relief Policy: customer is 92 DPD. What happens?
Referred to SAM (Bucket 4 & above excluded from Relief).
Q18. After Relief, the customer has paid 8 regular instalments and income is the same. Can a new PF be approved?
No – requires 12 regular payments (or 3 if income improved, with the Relief contract settled).
Q19. Expat age at maturity would be 63; policy maximum is 60. Which deviation level?
3 years over → more than 2 (Level 2) but within 5 → Level 3.
Q20. Who can approve a credit/documentation deviation for a PF of AED 3 million outside PDD?
CCO – within the AED 4 million delegation, with proper justification and business-head recommendation.
