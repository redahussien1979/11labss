Personal Finance – Segments Tree

DIB Personal Finance Operating Credit Policy (ref. 2026-07-0005 | 002306) + Credit Circular effective 15 Feb 2026 · Max finance figures in AED · "STL" = DIB salary, "PDC" = salary in another bank
PERSONAL FINANCE (DIB)
① SALARY WITH DIB

STL segment

  • Who: TML A, B, C + STL in DIB format; DIB Staff (as Cat A); Wajaha salaried; Ayaan ≥ 12M
  • Salaries: 3, latest in DIB (A/B/C waivers a–e)
  • Min income: 3,000
  • Max: Local 5M · Expat 3M
FreshLCLSSukuk Pack

Salaried / Privileged

  • Who: TML Cat D or Non-TML
  • Salaries: 3 (any bank), latest in DIB
  • Min income: 5,000
  • Max Local: D 2M · NTM 800K
  • Max Expat: D 1M · NTM 500K (low/very low) / 200K (med/high)
FreshLCLS*Sukuk Pack
*LS: Cat D enabled (circular); Non-TML only after system readiness
② SALARY IN OTHER BANK (PDC)

PDC – TML (A/B/C/D)

  • Salaries: 3 (1 or more banks)
  • Min income: 5,000
  • Max: Local 800K · Expat 300K
  • DDA mandatory
FreshLCSukuk Pack

PDC – Non-TML

  • Min income: 5,000
  • Max: Local 200K · Expat 150K
  • Fraud score applies
FreshLC not allowedSukuk Pack

Watch

  • AECB score < 541 in PDC → reject
  • UAE Nat 18–21 → PDC not allowed
③ SECURED / RELATIONSHIP

Against TD / Investment

  • DIB individuals · FD/TD/SIP/cash · AED or USD
  • Third-party deposit → personal guarantee
  • Max: 100M Wajaha/Ayaan · 10M others
  • % of block (principal + profit): income ≤60K p.a. 50% · >60K 100% · SE 100% · non-resident 95% · SIP-3D 90%
  • No DBR · no security cheque · min age 18

Private Banking (7.6)

  • Wajaha (relationship nil) · Ayaan/Affluent (12M) · Mumayyaz
  • 20x assessed income
  • Max: Wajaha 1M · Ayaan 500K
  • Max age 75 · lien non-mandatory
④ BUNDLED WITH ANOTHER PRODUCT

Auto Sukuk Pack (7.3)

  • AF + PF together, same KYC/income docs, non-scorecard
  • % of car: STL 30% · PDC 20% · Non-TML 20%
  • Exposure: TML 200K · Non-TML 50K
  • Min salary 3K (PDC Non-TML 10K, 5 dealers only)

Home Finance Sukuk Pack (7.4)

  • Finances HF ancillary costs only (fees & charges)
  • HFCA approves · primary customer only · no new AECB · 1st EMI ≤ 90 days

Asset Cross Sell (7.5)

  • System PF limit with AF / Covered Card
  • DIB salary, TML A/B/C · income ≥ 8K · PF ≥ 10K
  • PF max: Expat 350K · National 1M (< asset value)
  • Aggregate: Expat 500K · National 1M · valid 30 days
⑤ REMEDIAL

Finance Payment Relief (7.7)

  • Existing DIB facility · income dropped · DBR above CBUAE limit
  • Up to 89 DPD (Bucket 4+ → SAM)
  • Salary within 40 days · liability ≥ 10K
  • All PF + AF + cards → 1 Relief facility
  • Tenure no cap · no cash · no fees
  • AECB "Arrangement" · ECL Stage 2
  • Max age Expat 65 · UAE Nat no cap
  • New finance after 12 payments
⑥ CLOSED / RESTRICTED

Self-Employed

  • Stopped Oct 2022
  • 8.3 grid only for RCD deviation: Local 4.5x / Expat 3.5x; max 350K / 250K

Restricted profiles

  • Bedouin / Stateless
  • With Marsoom
  • Domestic workers (individual visa)
  • FI outsource staff (all segments)
  • Non-TML outsource
  • Employer code 8 (everyone) · code 2 (Expats)
  • Bank's external auditors
