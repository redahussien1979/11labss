POLICY SECTION 7.5

Asset Cross Sell

POLICY TEXTPolicy pages 11–12
Based on pre-defined eligibility parameters in Finnone, Personal Finance (PF) limit will be generated by system when customer is applying for Auto Finance or Covered Card from DIB.
During credit evaluation for Auto Finance or Covered Card (the primary product), the Finnone system calculates eligibility for PF. Credit Analysts can prevent Finnone from generating a PF limit
If eligible, a separate PF application is submitted through the primary product, bypassing RCD and CPV stages and proceeding directly to PDOC for affordability assessment. The secondary product is offered independently, with a separate contract allowing the customer to decline it without any pressure, in line with Consumer Protection Standard 5.1.1.78.
  1. Acceptable employer and segment for PF cross sell when customer applies for AF or CC
    1. 1.1. Working for PF TML Cat A, B & C and having salary transfer relationship with DIB. TML remarks or specified under restriction section applicable
  2. Stateless are not allowed
  3. Age: Minimum 21 and maximum 60 for expat and 65 for UAE nationals at maturity
  4. Exclusions: When any of the following in the primary product is met then PF limit should not be generated
    1. 4.1. If Finnone remarks with any code (from 1 to 9)
    2. 4.2. If customer has been issued liability letter or clearance letter in last 120 days
    3. 4.3. If customer already has an active PF under Liability Settlement Type 1 scheme
    4. 4.4. Joint account holder
    5. 4.5. Customer with more than 1 returned cheque in last 3 months
    6. 4.6. Primary product is approved under deviation
    7. 4.7. Pensioner
    8. 4.8. Previously closed card where Account's "ADMINISTRATIVE_STATUS_DESC" (a status field in Power Card) is as follows:
    9. 4.9. COLLECTION PERMANENT BLOCK or COLLECTION TEMPORARY BLOCK
    10. 4.10. Exclusions : When customer is applying for primary product and segment is from the list then system will not generate limit for PF
      1. 4.10.1. Any finance against TD or Cash – AF & CC
      2. 4.10.2. DIB Staff – AF & CC
      3. 4.10.3. Any Non-Individual finance such as Company finance or self-employer – CC & AF
      4. 4.10.4. Any Cross Sell segment (Segment name as AUTO/PF Bundling, CASA Bundling etc) – CC
      5. 4.10.5. Any Non-DIB Salary Transfer segment (PDC TML or Non TML) – CC & AF
      6. 4.10.6. WAJAHA / Private Banking, Salaried and Self Employed – CC & AF
      7. 4.10.7. MY HOME – CC
      8. 4.10.8. BUSINESS CARD and Self Employed Segment – CC
      9. 4.10.9. PAYROLL – CC
      10. 4.10.10. Any Non TML Segment – CC
      11. 4.10.11. CORPORATE CARD – CC
      12. 4.10.12. Any Pre-Approved Segment – CC & AF PREAPPROVED ASSET/STL – CC
      13. 4.10.13. Privileged Segment – AF
  5. Minimum assessed income : AED 8,000
  6. Minimum Finance Limit: AED 10,000
  7. Maximum Exposure: AED 350,000 Expat and AED 1,000,000 National. These limits must be less than the primary product's asset value
  8. Maximum Multiples: 20x or 15x for MOD inclusive of existing proposed PF. The aggregate multiples of all existing and new PF facility to remain within 15 multiples for MOD and 20 for Non-MOD
  9. Maximum Tenure: For Non-MOD: 48 Months and for MOD: 60 months
  10. Max. DBR: 50% and 30% for Pensioners

    3. 11.3. Copy of valid Emirates ID
    4. 11.4. Income documents
    5. 11.5. Customers with first time PF must provide STL as per DIB format
  12. Approval Validity: Up to 30 days from primary product last credit approval
  13. Eligibility Calculation
    1. 13.1. Total Aggregate exposure = Total Outstanding balance + primary product finance amount + existing covered card limit + secondary product finance amount + secondary product Covered card limit
    2. 13.2. Aggregate exposure of AF + CC and PF should not exceed AED 1,000,000 for UAE Nationals & 500,000 for Expats
IN SIMPLE WORDS
  • Only for DIB-salaried TML Cat A, B, C customers applying for AF or a Covered Card.
  • The PF is a separate contract the customer can freely refuse (Consumer Protection Standard 5.1.1.78). It skips RCD and CPV and goes to PDOC for affordability.
  • No PF limit if: any Finnone remarks code 1–9, liability/clearance letter in the last 120 days, active PF under LS Type 1, joint account holder, > 1 returned cheque in 3 months, primary product approved under deviation, pensioner, previous card with COLLECTION PERMANENT/TEMPORARY BLOCK status, or the primary-product segment is on the 4.10 exclusion list.
  • Key numbers: income ≥ 8,000; PF ≥ 10,000; PF exposure ≤ 350,000 (Expat) / 1,000,000 (National) and below the primary product's asset value; total multiples (all PFs) ≤ 20x (15x MOD); tenure 48 (60 MOD); DBR 50% (30% pensioners); approval valid 30 days.
TRAINER NOTE
Rule 4.7 excludes pensioners, while rule 10 still states a 30% DBR for pensioners. Follow the exclusion (no cross-sell PF limit for pensioners) and confirm with RCPPM.

If the same customer requested PF AED 200,000 → fails check 2 (not below the car's asset value of AED 180,000).
