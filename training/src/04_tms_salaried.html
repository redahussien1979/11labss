Payment History with DIB Facilities

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 8
  1. Scorecard segments:
    1. No current overdue check (internal with DIB) for Low & Very Low Risk
    2. Current overdue of > AED 1000 on cards & > AED 2000 on installment finances for Medium & High-Risk
      • System development is under way for point a & b, until this is in place the current overdue check will be done as current bucket level and system will stop an application if current bucket is > 0
    3. Never bucket 4 in last 12 months
  2. Non-Scored segments:
    1. Current overdue: Nil. System check is done on current bucket and stops the case when current bucket <> 0
    2. Bucket 2: Against Term Deposit 2 times and 1 time for other segments
    3. Bucket 3 & above: Never in the last 12 months
  3. Rescheduled Accounts
    1. Rescheduled > 12months – Auto pass
    2. Rescheduled 0-6 months for Expat – Auto Reject
    3. All others reschedule scenarios continue to be referred
Remarks
  • Maximum last 12 months' payment history check on all existing/run off retail product
  • Based on valid justification and collection system's feedback, any payment behavior may be considered by Initiation Manager & above for handling cases on exception
  • Same bucket repeated in 2 consecutive months will be counted as 1, same bucket repeated > 2 times will all be counted twice
  • No incremental facilities would be approved for stage 3 customers. A list of customers have been uploaded on the RCD negative list to restrict further finance to such customers
IN SIMPLE WORDS
A "bucket" is the number of instalments overdue (Bucket 1 ≈ 1–29 days past due, Bucket 2 ≈ 30–59, Bucket 3 ≈ 60–89, Bucket 4 ≈ 90+; the policy itself links Bucket 3 to "< 90 DPD"). The bank looks back at the last 12 months on all existing and closed (run-off) retail products.
  • Today (until the new system logic is live): any current bucket > 0 stops the application in both scored and non-scored segments.
  • Scored: never Bucket 4 in the last 12 months.
  • Non-scored: Bucket 2 allowed max 2 times (against TD) or 1 time (other segments); Bucket 3+ never.
  • Rescheduled more than 12 months ago → auto pass; Expat rescheduled 0–6 months ago → auto reject; everything else → referred.
  • Stage 3 customers (on the RCD negative list) → no incremental facilities.
TRAINER NOTE
The bucket-to-days ranges in the explanation are the standard industry convention, given only for understanding; the policy defines only "Bucket 3 (i.e., < 90 DPD)".

Security Cheque

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 9
  1. The security cheque amount must not exceed 120% of the Net Finance Value and must be dated. The date should follow the bank's internal policies, with 1 to 3 cheques collected depending on the finance tenure contactless or Digital journeys: e-cheque is not required, applicable to both Expats and UAE Nationals
  2. Security cheque is not required from Cash Secured Finance
WORKED EXAMPLE
Net finance value AED 150,000 → each security cheque ≤ AED 180,000 (120%), dated as per internal policy, 1–3 cheques depending on tenure. Digital/contactless journey → no e-cheque needed. PF against TD (cash secured) → no security cheque.

Returned Cheques in DIB or Returned DDA – for Non-Scored Segment

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 9
  1. TML Cat A, B & C Under STL including LS & Against TD: 2 returned cheques/DDA of insufficient funds in recent past 3 months excluding Liability Settlement
  2. All Others: 1 returned cheque or DDA due to insufficient funds in recent past 3 months for other segments
Remarks
  1. It is acceptable to consider 1 count if same DDA is returned within 7 working days for normal cases and up to 29 days for Liability Settlement
  2. Same CHEQUE returned more than 1 time will be counted the times it is bounced
    • Not to be counted if returned instrument is settled within 7 days and documentary proof such as cash receipt/invoice with instrument copy original sighting by source
    • Cash receipt/invoice should identify the bounced cheque amount and cheque number for verification
WORKED EXAMPLE
Customer AB (STL, TML Cat B, non-scored case): 2 returned DDAs (insufficient funds) in the last 3 months → within limit (max 2). A third → exceeds.
Customer AC (PDC): the same DDA was presented on 3 May and again on 8 May (within 7 working days) and returned both times → counted as 1.
Customer AD: one cheque bounced 3 times → counted as 3 – unless it was settled within 7 days with a cash receipt identifying the amount and cheque number (original sighted by source) → then not counted.
