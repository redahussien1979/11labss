POLICY SECTION 8 – ANNEXURES

8.1 Documentation and General Guidelines

KYC Documents

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 17
For Existing to Bank (ETB) customers
If KYC in core is valid, the KYC check at RCD will not be done if sales does not change any field mentioned. Also, for ETB customers with valid (not expired) KYC, documents need not be uploaded in Finnone as the same is available in document management system (DMS). For ETB customers with expired KYC in core, information to be updated by Sales and document should be uploaded in Finnone. The modified fields will be highlighted as ORANGE at DDEQC stage and Credit Analyst to do the QC on the ORANGE highlighted fields only

EFR applicability: For NTB & ETB PDC and for LS NTB, the EFR is mandatory at QDE stage. For STL and LS ETB customers, EFR will not be done if KYC is valid else EFR will be done at PDOC stage if KYC has expired
Wherever EFR is mandatory and it is not successfully performed due to technical reason, the ID validation process will be as per Compliance's guidance in line with the CDD/EDD Documentation Checklist and Operational Risk.

Reference: https://customerfirst.dib.ae/en/KnowledgeCentre/Documents/CDD-EDD-Documentation-Checklist.pdf
Passport Copy: Wherever required, copy of valid Passport and visa page*. PP stamped by employer does not require original sighting
*Visa page is not required from UAE Nationals, GCC nationals and expats who have latest Emirates ID issued after April 10, 2022 having employer name. For Golden visa, sponsor/employer can be identified through any legitimate source/document such as Salary Transfer Letter (STL)/Salary Certificate (SC)/Pay slip, WPS/FTS transaction narration.

UAE ID Copy: Copy of valid UAE ID of the applicant/signatories in case of Non-Individual must be provided. PDC customers having UAE PP and having different nationality on Emirates ID, nationality can be considered as UAE National only in case of an error in DDA acceptance
  1. In case UAEID is under issuance/renewal/replacement (replacement against lost ID), the passport shall be used for identification purpose and a copy of the passport (original seen) along with the UAEID application form shall be submitted with every finance application. The application should not be more than 21 days
  2. GCC nationals holding UAE ID can provide proof of UAE residency in order to be treated as residents of the UAE for the banking transactions (current accounts/obtaining finances etc.)
  3. Non-residents and diplomats who are exempt from the issuance of a UAE ID are not required to provide it. Diplomats should submit a copy of their identification card issued by the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
  4. (item 4 is blank in the source)
AECB Consent: Customer consent will be taken by sales and validated by sales supervisor under a maker-checker process. No validation will be done by the RCD analyst
IN SIMPLE WORDS
  • ETB with valid KYC and no field changed → no KYC check at RCD and no document upload (documents already in DMS).
  • ETB with expired KYC → Sales updates and uploads; changed fields show ORANGE at DDEQC; the analyst QCs only the orange fields.
  • EFR (mandatory at QDE) for NTB (new-to-bank) and ETB PDC customers, and for LS NTB. For STL and LS ETB customers: no EFR if KYC is valid; EFR at PDOC if KYC expired.
  • Visa page not needed for UAE Nationals, GCC nationals, and expats whose latest EID was issued after 10 April 2022 and shows the employer name.
  • EID under renewal → passport (original seen) + EID application form, application not older than 21 days.
  • AECB consent: taken by Sales, checked by the Sales supervisor (maker-checker). RCD does not validate it.
TRAINER NOTE
"EFR", "QDE", "NTB" and "PDOC" are not defined in the policy's acronym list; they are system/process stage names used in DIB's workflow. NTB = New to Bank is the opposite of ETB (Existing to Bank) used in the same paragraph.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Customer AQ, Indian national, Emirates ID issued 3 March 2023 showing the employer name → visa page not required.
Customer AR, Expat, EID issued in 2021 → passport copy and visa page required.
Customer AS has lost the EID; the replacement application form is dated 10 days ago → use the passport (original seen) + EID application form → acceptable (≤ 21 days). If the form were 25 days old → not acceptable.

Income Document – Salary Transfer Letter (STL)

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 17
DIB Staff: No income documents
Emirates Group: Special arrangement with EK, STL is sent by EK to DIB Branches Support unit
Govt. & Semi Govt.: STL as per Government/employer format
  • Income Document requirements for fresh cases from Government & Semi Government
    1. If no salary in DIB: SC to be addressed to DIB & should contain IBAN/full A/C. Salary count to be considered as 1
    2. If < 3 salaried in DIB: SC addressed to "To Whom It May Concern" or to DIB is acceptable
    3. If ≥ 3 salaries in DIB: Only employee ID is required. If salary is irregular then SC shall be required
Private TML: STL as per DIB format unless exception is approved by the RCP&PM or the management. STL should contain
  1. Applicant name / designation / date of joining / salary details
  2. Designation of signatory and should cover following undertaking from employer suitably
    1. Transfer monthly salary into DIB account and not to transfer salary to any other bank or pay it directly to the employee unless DIB clearance is provided
    2. To inform DIB in case of employee's resignation/termination
    3. To transfer EOSB to employees account and not pay to employee directly thru cash/cheque
Exemptions: SC in lieu of STL in below scenarios
  1. For LC: SC addressed to "To Whom It May Concern" or to DIB is acceptable subject to no conflict with point "5". SC under LC is not required from local working with Govt.
  2. STL from same employer is provided in an earlier case/recently cancelled case
  3. Employer issues limited number of STL and won't issue fresh
  4. Employers that specify finance amount STL or any conditions in which STL may be mandatory, fresh STL may be required
  5. Income document is valid as long as credit approval is valid
Document Validity
  1. 30 days at the time of submission from TML Private Employers and as per validity mentioned on income document from Government, Semi Government
  2. For cross sell purpose, validity of the documents is Up to 60 days for additional product for DIB salaried segment
WORKED EXAMPLE – GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEE, FRESH CASE
Salaries already in DIBIncome document needed
0SC addressed to DIB with IBAN / full account number; salary count treated as 1
1 or 2SC addressed "To Whom It May Concern" or to DIB
3 or more (regular)Only the employee ID
3 or more (irregular salary)SC required
A private TML employer's STL dated 1 May submitted on 25 May → valid (within 30 days). Submitted on 5 June → expired (more than 30 days).

Income Document – Salary Certificate (SC)

POLICY TEXTPolicy pages 17–18
  1. Salary Certificate should contain following information
    1. Customer Name / Designation / Date of Joining / Salary Details
    2. Should be on company's letterhead/stationary and should have signing authority name, designation and company's stamp. SC should preferably be signed by Owner, Accounts, Admin or HR/Pay roll department officials. SC signed by owner from a Non TML employer is not acceptable if he/she is the spouse/sibling/parent/child of the customer
  2. Salaried/Privileged & PDC
    1. Should be addressed to customer's salary transferred bank or to DIB
    2. To Whom It May Concern or to any other parties but non-financial Institutions
  3. Source should provide clarification/proof in case there is a difference in customer designation on SC and Visa and same for the employer
  4. Income document is valid as long as credit approval is valid
  5. For Cross Sell
    1. Under Salaried/Privileged segment: SC validity is 60 days from its issuance date or as per validity mentioned in SC. If validity elapsed, then fresh SC is required
    2. Under PDC relationship: SC validity is 30 days from its issuance date or as per validity mentioned in SC. If validity elapsed, then fresh SC is required
Exemptions: SC under liability consolidation is not required from Local working with Govt.
WATCH OUT
A salary certificate from a Non-TML company signed by the owner who is the customer's spouse, sibling, parent or child is not acceptable. Designation/employer differences between SC and visa must be explained with proof by the source.

For Liability Settlement

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 18 (numbering as printed)
  1. 1. Liability letter (LL): LL is not acceptable if addressed to other banks or to DIWAN or to any government entity that settles UAE national debts. Moreover, liability letter issued from Collections unit of retiring bank is acceptable as long as customer has been paying regularly his liabilities for the past 6 months
  2. 2. Conditional approval without liability letter or with expired one
    1. Source may request maximum finance amount without or with expired liability letter or based on confirmation from customer for the tentative liabilities including CC outstanding. Source remains responsible & accountable for the genuineness of deals/customer interest.
    2. RCD can provide conditional approval with maximum amount to arrange document in advance to save TAT. Final approval will be based on submission of valid liability letter
    3. Post approval, RCD shall backstage the case to source for scanning the actual liability letter and for updating liabilities as per liability letter. Source shall provide fresh liability letter and input liabilities along with undertaking details as per fresh liability and guarantee letter, are-sent case back to Credit for final approval
  3. 3. The liability towards CC:
    1. Guarantee not to be conserved as liability, RCD to approve finance without the guarantee against card
      1. Liability only on CC: With/without salary transfer bank,. For existing DIB salary transfer customers, requesting to settle external liability of CC, then latest CC statement to be provided. The existing DSR will be calculated on CC Limit, and the settlement will be based on the outstanding balance in the CC statement, clearance/NOC letter will be required. No mandatory check on card utilization
  4. 2) DDA consent from customer will be a mandatory document requirement for PF Liability
    1. Settlement cases
  5. 4. For Internal Liability Settlement cases;
    1. If external liability is a mix of CC and finances, then the existing documents/process of LS to be followed
    2. One external liability mandatory to be settled (CC, PF, Auto)
    3. Liability letter mandatory, If LL is not available the full credit limit will be used in DSR calculation and settlement will be done on outstanding only.
    4. Internal liabilities can be settled (PF and Auto only). Liability consolidation minimum requirement is applicable as per exiting process
    5. DIB Covered Card not to be settled through this proposition, however exception may be allowed subject to Head of Card approval
    6. Clearance letter mandatory to be submitted by channel for cases where external liability is being settled via Liability letter so that additional amount can be released and stage can be changed to "End"
  6. 5. Release of additional amount blocked: can be released automatically after receiving STL at RAO and first salary in DIB A/C provided the salary credit covers at least EMI amount or as per exceptions allowed in RAO process
IN SIMPLE WORDS
  • The liability letter must be addressed properly – not to other banks, not to DIWAN, not to a government debt-settlement entity. An LL from the retiring bank's Collections unit is fine if the customer paid regularly for the last 6 months.
  • To save time, RCD can give a conditional approval (maximum amount) before the LL arrives; final approval only after a valid LL is scanned and liabilities updated.
  • Card-only buy-out: provide latest card statement; DSR uses the card limit; settlement uses the statement outstanding; clearance/NOC needed.
  • Internal LS: at least one external liability must be settled; internal PF and Auto can be included; a DIB covered card only with Head of Card approval.
  • The extra cash (amount above the settled liabilities) stays blocked until RAO receives the STL and the first salary arrives in DIB and covers at least the EMI.
WORKED EXAMPLE – CARD-ONLY SETTLEMENT
Customer AT (DIB salary transfer) wants DIB to settle an external credit card Card limit AED 40,000; statement outstanding AED 25,000 DSR uses: card LIMIT (40,000) Settlement amount: OUTSTANDING (25,000) Documents: latest card statement + clearance/NOC letter + DDA consent (PF liability settlement)
If an internal LS case has no liability letter, the full credit limit is used in DSR and the settlement is made on the outstanding only.

General Guidelines – Monthly Instalment Date & EMI Recovery

POLICY TEXTPolicy page 18
Monthly Installment Date:
  • Installment date not to be set for 1st or 2nd date of a month
  • DIB Salaried: Up to 5 days from the salary credit day/the two repeated date in last 3 months if salary is irregular
  • PDC: +1 day to the date of the salary credit day/the two repeated date in last 3 months if salary is irregular
EMI Recovery: Direct debit from DIB or through DDA from Non-DIB account
WORKED EXAMPLE
DIB-salaried customer, salary credited on the 25th → EMI date can be set from the 25th up to the 30th (up to 5 days).
PDC customer, salary credited on the 27th at the other bank → EMI date = 28th (+1 day), recovered by DDA.
PDC customer with salary credited on the 31st → +1 day would fall on the 1st of next month, which is not allowed; the case needs RCD guidance.
TRAINER NOTE
The policy does not state what to do when the "+1 day" lands on the 1st or 2nd. Confirm the practice with RCD.
