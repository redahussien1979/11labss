Mind Map – the whole policy on one page

Start at the centre and go clockwise from top-left: Who → Where → How much → Limits, then Checks → Schemes → Special products → Relief & restricted. Redraw it from memory on blank paper to test yourself.

① WHO & AGE

  • Min age 21 (18 cash-secured)
  • UAEN 18–21: DIB salary, lower of 10x / 250K, no PDC
  • Max at maturity: Expat 60 (65 if ≥ 30K)
  • UAEN 65 · pension only 75 · Private Banking 75
  • GCC nationals = Expat

② WHERE IS THE SALARY? → SEGMENT

  • DIB + TML A/B/C + STL → STL
  • DIB + TML D / Non-TML → Salaried/Privileged
  • Other bank → PDC TML (A–D) / PDC NTML
  • 3 salaries, latest in DIB (DIB segments)
  • DIB staff = Cat A · BFI = Cat B

③ HOW MUCH? (min income & max)

  • Min income: DIB A/B/C 3K · D/NTML 5K · PDC 5K
  • Local: DIB 5M · 2M · 800K · PDC 800K · 200K
  • Expat: DIB 3M · 1M · 500K/200K · PDC 300K · 150K
  • Contactless: Local 750K · Expat 350K
  • Min finance 10K fresh · 5K top-up

④ CBUAE HARD LIMITS

  • DBR 50% salary · 30% pension
  • Multiples 20x · 15x MOD
  • Tenure 48M · 60M MOD (min 6M)
  • Security cheque ≤ 120% · HF applicant: no PF 2 months
  • Final amount = LOWEST cap

⑤ CHECKS (AECB & history)

  • Auto reject: score 300 · PDC < 541 · Expat overdue > 10K
  • Current bucket > 0 → stop · never B4 in 12M
  • Expat reject multiples: High 0x; Medium 12/14/20-15/20x
  • Returned cheques 3M: 2 (STL A/B/C, LS, TD) · 1 others
  • Codes: 8 all restricted · 2 Expats restricted

⑥ SCHEMES

  • Fresh (min 10K)
  • LC top-up: after 12M; earlier if < 20x used or income +AED 200; NTML 3 EMIs
  • LS buy-out: TML A–D + staff · settled facility ≤ 29 DPD
  • No LC for PDC Non-TML
  • Sukuk Pack: with AF/HF, same docs

⑦ SPECIAL PRODUCTS

  • TD: 50/100/95/90% of block · 100M / 10M
  • Auto Sukuk: 30% STL / 20% PDC & NTML · 200K/50K
  • HF Sukuk: ancillary costs only · 1st EMI ≤ 90 days
  • Cross sell: income 8K · PF 350K/1M · aggregate 500K/1M · 30 days
  • Private Banking: 20x · Wajaha 1M · Ayaan 500K

⑧ RELIEF & RESTRICTED

  • Relief: ≤ 89 DPD · salary ≤ 40 days · liability ≥ 10K
  • All facilities → 1 · no cap · no cash · no fees · Arrangement, Stage 2
  • New finance after 12 payments
  • Closed: Self-Employed (Oct 2022)
  • Restricted: Stateless, Marsoom, domestic workers, outsource, auditors

21-Day Spaced Repetition Plan

Short, spaced sessions beat one long session. Review just before you would forget.

Rules that make it work: (1) Always try to answer before looking. (2) A card is "known" only if every number is correct. (3) Missed cards come back sooner – known cards come back later. (4) Explain a rule to a colleague in your own words – teaching is the strongest test. (5) When a new credit circular is issued, update the matching card the same day.
