SELF-CHECK

Quiz – 20 questions with answers

Q1. A Kuwaiti national applies for PF. Is the customer treated as UAE National or Expat?
Expat – GCC nationals are categorised as Expat (section 4).
Q2. A self-employed doctor applies for a normal PF. What is the decision?
Not eligible – Self-Employed segment stopped in October 2022 (only RCD deviation using Annexure 8.3).
Q3. TML Cat B customer, STL in DIB format, no salary in DIB yet, salary certificate verified via a government portal. Can the first-salary requirement be waived?
Yes – criterion b) STL/SC Validation (A/B/C only).
Q4. PDC customer working for a Non-TML company asks for Liability Consolidation. Allowed?
No – "Liability Consolidation for Non TML under PDC is not allowed".
Q5. UAE National, 20 years old, DIB salary AED 15,000. Maximum finance?
Lower of 10 × 15,000 = 150,000 or 250,000 → AED 150,000 (STL only, PDC not allowed).
Q6. Expat, PDC, AECB score 530. Result?
Auto reject – PDC with score < 541.
Q7. Salary AED 18,000, existing obligations AED 4,000. Maximum new monthly instalment under DBR?
50% × 18,000 = 9,000 − 4,000 = AED 5,000.
Q8. MOD employee, salary AED 20,000. Maximum multiples and tenure?
15x (AED 300,000) and 60 months.
Q9. Expat, Medium risk, salary AED 6,500, wants 15x. Decision?
Band ≥5K–<8K allows 14x → 15x is more than specified → auto-reject (Note 2). A +2 multiples deviation (Level 2) would be needed.
Q10. Expat, DIB salary AED 40,000. Maximum payment holiday for the first EMI?
180 days (DIB salary incl. LS, income ≥ 35K).
Q11. Finance AED 300,000. Maximum security cheque value?
120% × 300,000 = AED 360,000.
Q12. Salaried customer with annual income AED 50,000 blocks a TD of AED 80,000. Maximum PF (principal + profit)?
Annual income ≤ 60K → 50% → AED 40,000 including profit.
Q13. Car price AED 120,000, PDC TML customer. Maximum Sukuk Pack?
20% × 120,000 = AED 24,000 (within AED 200,000 exposure cap).
Q14. Home Finance Sukuk Pack: should PF Sales pull a new AECB report?
No – AECB is already pulled by the HF team (7.4.5 / 7.4.8.1).
Q15. A customer applies for a Covered Card and is a pensioner. Will Finnone generate a cross-sell PF limit?
No – pensioners are excluded (7.5, 4.7).
Q16. Ayaan customer with 10 months' relationship. Eligible for Private Banking PF?
Policy minimum is 12 months → not eligible at Level 1; 2 months short is within the Level 2 deviation (3Ms below policy).
Q17. Relief Policy: customer is 92 DPD. What happens?
Referred to SAM (Bucket 4 & above excluded from Relief).
Q18. After Relief, the customer has paid 8 regular instalments and income is the same. Can a new PF be approved?
No – requires 12 regular payments (or 3 if income improved, with the Relief contract settled).
Q19. Expat age at maturity would be 63; policy maximum is 60. Which deviation level?
3 years over → more than 2 (Level 2) but within 5 → Level 3.
Q20. Who can approve a credit/documentation deviation for a PF of AED 3 million outside PDD?
CCO – within the AED 4 million delegation, with proper justification and business-head recommendation.
